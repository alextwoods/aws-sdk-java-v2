## Decision applied: Applied (re-implemented here) (KEEP)

Both targets gained beyond their floors: v2-async small-get saved 2,445 B/op (−4.3%, floor 0.2%) and v2-async
small-put 2,415 B/op (−4.7%, floor 0.3%). No guard regressed beyond its floor, and no run failed (140/140 in the A/B).
The v2-async describe-table guard saved about the same amount (2,433 B/op, −3.7%), which fits a saving per CRT
request, independent of the operation. The v2-sync arm uses Apache5 and does not load `CrtRequestAdapter`; its B/op
moved by +0.2% and +0.3% on small-get and small-put, inside the 0.5% and 0.4% floors. One CPU metric moved beyond its
floor, in the improving direction: v2-async describe-table application CPU −3.0% against a 2.8% floor. Guards only
check regressions, and one case just past its floor is not a CPU claim. No other CPU metric moved beyond its floor.
`cand/E12` is merged into `perf/racecar-e2e`, and the row in `racecar-decisions.yaml` is now
`Applied (re-implemented here)` with `classes`, `applied_in_phase: 5` and this record.

For scale: the laptop smoke (`E12-smoke.md`, 2 × 300 operations) saw −2,236 B/op on v2-async small-get. On the metal
host at 7 × 50,000 operations the same scenario saved 2,445 B/op.
