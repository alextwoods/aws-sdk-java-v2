## Decision applied: Rejected (REVERT)

The keep rule needs every target to gain beyond its floor. Three of the four targets did: v2-sync small-get saved
309 B/op (floor 0.5%), v2-async small-get 506 B/op (floor 0.2%) and v2-async small-put 622 B/op (floor 0.3%). The
v2-sync small-put target saved 146 B/op (−0.3%), inside its 0.4% floor. No guard regressed beyond its floor, and no
CPU metric moved beyond its floor in either direction. The record's suggestion is Rejected, and the row in
`racecar-decisions.yaml` is now `Rejected` with these numbers. `cand/B1` is not merged.

For scale: the laptop smoke (`B1-smoke.md`, 2 × 300 operations) saw −815 B/op on v2-sync small-get. On the metal host
at 7 × 50,000 operations the same scenario saved 309 B/op, 0.6% of 53.4 KB/op.
